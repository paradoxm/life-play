# Жизнь (Life)

**Play:** https://paradoxm.github.io/life-play/

*Life* is a browser life simulator built on official statistics. You are born where real babies are born — any of 214 countries, any year from 1850 to today, weighted by how many children were actually born there and then — and you live one life that never repeats: childhood, school, work, love, family, money, illness, wars, migration, old age and death.

Every scene follows the numbers of its country and era: life expectancy, child mortality, schooling, incomes and prices in the local currency, housing, marriage, war and migration. Statistics come from public sources such as the World Bank, WHO, UNICEF, ILO, UNESCO, UN DESA, UNHCR and Our World in Data, and a scene often shows the real figure behind it.

- **Your choices and the world together.** Decisions shape a life, but so do where and when you were born, history, and luck.
- **History that happened.** Real wars since 1850, and the pandemics, famines, crashes and collapses of the 20th and 21st centuries, reach the people who lived through them.
- **Life goes on in your children.** After death you can continue as one of your children.
- **For phones.** Install it to the home screen; after one visit it plays offline.
- **18+.** The game speaks plainly about poverty, violence, loss and death.

The game is in Russian; other languages are planned.
